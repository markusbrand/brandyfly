## Why

The map's "Thermal Updraft Hotspots" overlay currently draws a hardcoded list of mock points (`defaultMockThermalHotspots` in `map_widget.dart`). It gives pilots no real information about where to expect lift. thermal.kk7.ch publishes statistically derived thermal probability heatmaps, filtered by season and time of day, and the source is already listed as approved in `docs/data_source_governance.md`. Pilots often lose connectivity in the mountains, so the heatmap must be available offline for the areas they fly. Otherwise it fails at the moment they need it.

## What Changes

- Replace the mock thermal hotspot markers with the KK7 thermal probability raster heatmap (`thermals_<season>_<time>`), rendered as a MapLibre raster layer between the base map and the airspace/track overlays.
- The existing map widget toggle `mapShowThermals` ("Thermal Updraft Hotspots") switches the heatmap on and off. **BREAKING (visual only)**: the mock thermal dots are removed.
- Add per-map-widget configuration for thermal **season** (`auto`, `all`, `jan`, `apr`, `jul`, `oct`), **time of day** (`auto`, `all`, morning `04`, midday `07`, evening `10`) and heatmap **opacity**.
- Auto mode selects the KK7 variant on the device. Season comes from the calendar month, using KK7's meteorological quarters. Time of day comes from hours since local sunrise, computed offline from the pilot position and date. The layer switches automatically when the active variant changes during a flight.
- Serve thermal tiles through a new `/thermals/{variant}/{z}/{x}/{y}.png` route on the existing `LocalTileServer`. Resolution order: prefetched region store, persistent browse cache, KK7 online (with `src` tracking parameter), transparent tile.
- Add on-device thermal prefetch tied to downloaded map regions. Tiles up to zoom 12 are fetched for the region bounding box, for the current season and all four time variants. During the last month of a season, the next season is prefetched as well. Outdated seasons are pruned.
- Prefetch runs automatically when a region becomes available and when the season window changes, the next time the device is online. Pilots also get a per-region refresh/retry action, progress and size display, and a global opt-out toggle.
- Show KK7 attribution and license (`Thermal map © thermal.kk7.ch, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`) whenever the layer is visible.
- Correct the KK7 entry in `docs/data_source_governance.md` to reflect the actual license (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), the `src` parameter requirement, and the on-device (non-redistributing) fetch model.

### Non-goals

- No redistribution of KK7 tiles through BrandyFly servers, CDN, or region packages generated by the map pipeline.
- No KK7 skyways, certainty (`knowledges_*`) or vector hotspot (gpx/cup/csv) layers. These may follow later.
- No weather-aware or live thermal forecasting. The heatmap is statistical only.
- No use of the heatmap in audio, the Rust flight core, or thermal assistant logic.
- No arbitrary-radius or custom bounding-box prefetch independent of downloaded regions.
- No crossfading between season variants. KK7 already blends season borders inside its data.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `kk7-thermal-heatmap-layer`: Rendering, toggle, season/time-of-day variant selection (manual and auto), opacity, layer ordering, tile resolution order, and attribution for the KK7 thermal probability heatmap on the map widget.
- `thermal-tile-prefetch`: On-device prefetch, storage, season-window refresh, pruning, rate limiting, resume, opt-out and failure handling of KK7 thermal tiles for downloaded map regions.

### Modified Capabilities

- `offline-vector-map-rendering`: The flight overlay requirement no longer includes mock thermal updraft markers. Thermal information comes from the KK7 heatmap layer instead. The licensing attribution requirement is extended to KK7 when the thermal layer is visible.

## Impact

- **Code**: `apps/mobile/lib/ui/features/map/views/map_widget.dart` (remove mock thermal painting, add raster layer), `lib/services/maplibre_map_service.dart` (style source/layer injection), `lib/services/local_tile_server.dart` (new thermal route, persistent cache), `lib/domain/models/ui_config.dart` and `widget_config_sheet.dart` (season/time/opacity settings), new services for variant selection (sunrise calculation) and thermal prefetch.
- **Storage**: About 55 MB per catalog-sized region (alps-east) and season set, measured at about 6 KB per live KK7 tile, up to about double that during a season transition. Stored under `{appSupport}/regions/<id>/thermals/`, so deleting a region removes its thermal tiles. The browse cache moves out of the OS-purgeable temp directory for thermal tiles.
- **Network**: Anonymous HTTPS GET requests to `thermal.kk7.ch`, with the `src=brandyfly` parameter and a polite request rate. No pilot position or identity is sent. Tile coordinates covering a downloaded region are inherently area-revealing, the same as for base map tiles.
- **Offline / safety**: The heatmap is advisory only. Missing tiles render transparent and never block the map or flight instruments. Flight-critical functions are unaffected.
- **Licensing**: KK7 data is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Using it in this MIT-licensed, non-commercial open-source app is permitted with attribution. Tiles are not modified or redistributed. The KK7 author should be informed about the expected prefetch load.
- **Dependencies**: Works with existing `regions/<id>/map.pmtiles` by reading bounds from the PMTiles header. It integrates with `offline-region-download-manager` once that lands, using catalog bounds and download-completion hooks, but does not require it.
