## 2024-05-24 - Improve keyboard accessibility in Flutter TextField forms
**Learning:** In Flutter apps, text fields without explicit `textInputAction` and `onSubmitted` handlers leave users with unresolved keyboards, requiring manual dismissal which degrades UX.
**Action:** When designing or refactoring forms and search bars in Flutter, configure `TextField` widgets with appropriate `textInputAction` (e.g., `TextInputAction.search`, `TextInputAction.next`, `TextInputAction.done`) and bind form submission or keyboard dismissal (`FocusManager.instance.primaryFocus?.unfocus()`) to the `onSubmitted` callback.
