# BrandyFly User Guide

Welcome to BrandyFly! BrandyFly is an open-source, local-first paragliding vario application designed to assist you during your flights.

*Note: The project is currently in its foundation phase and does not yet provide flight-ready functionality.*

## Overview

BrandyFly aims to provide a robust, reliable flight instrument right on your mobile device (Android and iOS). By leveraging your device's sensors and combining them with a high-performance core and offline mapping, we strive to give you essential telemetry and situational awareness.

## Features (In Development)

*   **Local-First Architecture:** Designed to work entirely offline, ensuring you have access to critical data even without an internet connection in remote flying areas.
*   **High-Performance Vario:** Utilizing a deterministic Rust flight core for accurate and responsive climb/sink rate calculations.
*   **Offline Maps:** Integration with MapLibre for detailed, offline-capable mapping to aid in navigation and situational awareness.

## Safety Guidelines

**Crucial Warning:** BrandyFly is currently **not** suitable for navigation or flight safety decisions.

*   **Information Reliability:** Airspace and map data provided by the app may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate.
*   **Pilot Responsibility:** You, the pilot, remain solely responsible for your safety. Always use current, official information sources (like certified aeronautical charts) and appropriate, certified flight equipment for primary navigation and safety-critical decisions.
